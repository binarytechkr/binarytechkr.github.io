Binarytech provides Party Picker. Player names and game records stay on your device. Sections 1–5 cover Android; the iOS section covers the App Store version for iPhone and iPad, and the Apps in Toss section covers the version running inside Toss. Each feature disclosure applies when a version containing that feature becomes publicly available. Support and privacy rights apply to all distributions.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to Party Picker for Android, intended for people aged 13 and older. Binarytech is the operator and privacy contact: binarytech804@gmail.com. The app has no account registration, developer-operated game server, or separate analytics/crash-reporting SDK. Optional one-time ad removal is available through Google Play billing.

The Android 1.5.0 interstitial-ad and ad-cadence storage disclosures apply when that version becomes publicly available.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Player names, preferences, the current game and results: stored in app-specific storage for gameplay, recovery and recent results. These are not sent to a developer server or included in advertising requests.
  • Recent results: retained within the app's cleanup limits of 20 rounds or seven days. Preferences, names and an unfinished game may remain until reset.
  • Age information: we do not request or store dates of birth, age groups or individual participants' ages.
  • Advertising consent: Google UMP stores consent status and privacy choices locally and may send information needed to process consent to Google.
  • Ad removal purchase: a signed Google Play receipt, containing the product ID, purchase token, time and applicable order identifiers, is stored locally to maintain ad removal offline. Player names and game records are not linked to purchase information.
  • Review request record: only the time of the Google Play review request is stored on your device so the request is not repeated. Resetting game data does not remove it; clearing the app's storage or uninstalling does.

Interstitial-ad cadence record (from Android 1.5.0): the cycle counter for completed rounds, the last completed round identifier and the original installation time (or first launch time if unavailable) are stored on your device to manage ad intervals and avoid counting a round twice. These records are not sent to a developer server or advertising API, or transmitted with player names or game results. Resetting game data does not remove them; clearing Android app storage or uninstalling does.

3. Advertising and Google's data processing

Banner and interstitial ads: the Android version serves Google AdMob banners. From 1.5.0, it also serves an interstitial before revealing results each time four more rounds are newly completed. For 24 hours after the original installation, no interstitials or related notices are shown, and rounds played during that period do not count toward the ad interval. This grace period does not apply to banners. If installation time is unavailable, first launch time is used; updating an existing installation does not restart the grace period. The home screen shows the rounds remaining before the next interstitial, and the round due for an ad provides advance notice on the game screen and just before results. If an ad cannot be loaded, results are revealed without it. Verified ad removal stops both banners and interstitials.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Every ad request receives Google's CHILD age treatment. This does not determine a user's actual age or change the app's intended audience. Personalized ads, remarketing and requests to third-party ad vendors are restricted, and the Android Advertising Identifier (AAID) is not transmitted with this treatment. UMP separately receives an under-age-of-consent protection signal, which suppresses European advertising consent requests for the tagged users. Ads are requested only when the SDK reports they are eligible. Ad creatives are limited to a maximum G rating. Banners are hidden during gameplay.

When advertising is enabled, Google may collect and share the following for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it
  • Product and ad interactions, such as app launches, ad taps and views
  • Performance and diagnostic information, such as launch times and hangs
  • App set ID and applicable device/account identifiers (excluding Android advertising ID under this app's protective configuration)

SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. We do not attach player names, round details or game results to ad requests. The advertising provider's own analytics and diagnostics are separate from the absence of a dedicated developer analytics SDK.

4. Third parties and international processing

Sharing results: tapping “Share result to group chat” on the result screen opens the Android share sheet. The message contains the game name, the selected players’ names (or each group’s members), the number of players and a Google Play Store link. The link carries only fixed campaign values (such as utm_source=result_share) used to count shares, and nothing that identifies you. It is sent only after you choose the app and recipients, and the app does not send it to a developer server. The app you choose handles the message under its own policies. Please make sure the people named agree before you share.

Review requests: after two newly completed rounds, the app asks for a review once per install from the home screen using Google Play In-App Review. Google Play decides whether the review dialog appears, and the app does not receive ratings or whether a review was written. Ratings and reviews are published on Google Play with your Google Play account under Google’s policies.

Ad removal is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Google Play manages payment methods and Play account information; the app does not receive card numbers. The app uses the Play Billing Library to query and acknowledge purchases and restore them with the same Play account. Receipt signatures are verified in the app, and receipts are not sent to a developer server. Verified ownership stops banner and interstitial requests; ownership revocation reported by Play removes that entitlement. Payments, refunds and Google's transaction retention follow Google Play policies and applicable law.

Advertising and consent data is transmitted to Google's advertising services and may be processed by Google and its service providers on servers in the United States and other countries. Recipients, processing locations, retention periods and safeguards may vary with the service and consent choices. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner services for processing, retention, international transfers and rights procedures. We do not determine or guarantee Google's server retention periods.

5. Choices, retention and deletion

  • Reset local data in Settings resets player names, the current round and results. Advertising privacy preferences are managed separately by the SDK.
  • Resetting game data does not cancel ad removal. Clear Android app storage or uninstall to delete the local receipt; it can be restored with the same Play account. Deleting app data does not delete Google Play order records or refund the purchase.
  • Where required, use Advertising privacy options in Settings to review or change consent. This option appears only when the SDK requires it.
  • Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall to remove locally stored app data. Android settings also provide controls to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • The app is configured to exclude its data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Manufacturer-specific backup behavior is described by the device provider.
  • Resetting local game data does not delete data already processed by Google. Use Google's privacy tools and contact procedures to exercise rights relating to its data.

iOS (App Store): storage, advertising and purchases

This section applies to Party Picker installed from the App Store on iPhone or iPad. The version inside Toss follows the Apps in Toss section below regardless of the device's operating system. The iOS version has no separate account registration, developer-operated game server, or separate analytics/crash-reporting SDK.

  • Local game storage: player names, preferences, rounds in progress, results, solo records and solo progress are saved in app-specific storage. Solo games may show an interstitial before results after sufficient play time. Rewarded ads appear only when you choose a reward button. If an ad cannot load, basic gameplay and viewing results remain available. Verified ad removal stops banners and interstitials and provides rewards without ads.
  • Advertising data and consent: Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device/app identifiers, app and ad interactions, ads viewed, and crash/performance diagnostics for delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not attach player names, round content, scores or results to ad requests. Google UMP stores consent status and privacy choices locally and may send necessary information to Google. Every ad request receives CHILD protective treatment and a maximum G ad rating; UMP also receives an under-age-of-consent signal. These settings do not collect or determine your age. Requests are made only after the SDK reports they are eligible; advertising privacy options appear in Settings when required.
  • Tracking and IDFA: the current iOS version does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not use alternative identifiers to track you across other companies' apps or websites. ATT and regional advertising consent are separate; not using IDFA does not mean the advertising SDK performs no data processing. Device tracking choices can be managed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad cadence and review records: the ad cycle counter, last completed round identifier, installation or first-launch time, last interstitial time and review-request time are stored on your device to manage intervals and repeated requests. They are not sent to a developer server or advertising API and are retained separately from game-data resets.
  • App Store purchases and restoration: ad removal is an optional one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Apple manages payment methods and Apple Account information; the app does not receive card numbers. The app checks verified entitlements through StoreKit and stores purchase verification data, including transaction identifiers and signed transaction information, on your device. Purchase data is not linked to names or game records or sent to a developer server. Restore purchases in Settings checks purchases with the same Apple Account; a confirmed refund or revocation removes ad removal. Google Play and Toss entitlements are separate. Actual availability and prices are shown in the App Store purchase sheet.
  • Sharing results and reviews: choosing to share results opens the iOS share sheet. The message contains the game name, selected players' names (or each group's members), the number of players and an App Store link. The link contains no player names or user identifiers. You choose the receiving app and recipients; shared content follows that service's policies and is not sent to a developer server. Please make sure the people named agree before you share. After two newly completed rounds within one app execution, the app requests an App Store review once per install from the home screen. Apple decides whether the sheet appears; the app does not receive ratings or whether a review was written. Reviews follow Apple's policies.
  • Retention, deletion and backups: resetting data in Settings removes names, group-game records, solo records and progress. Advertising consent and cadence, review records and purchase information are managed separately; resetting games does not cancel purchases. Deleting the app in iOS Settings removes its local app-data copies. Offloading an app may retain data and differs from deleting it. The Documents directory containing game data is configured to be excluded from iOS backups; this does not mean every system or SDK preference is excluded. Deleting the app does not delete Apple's transaction records or data already processed by Google, or refund a payment.
  • Providers and international processing: Google handles advertising and consent data; Apple handles payment, purchase restoration and review information. Processing may take place in the United States and other countries. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for processing, retention, international transfers and rights. Local resets and provider deletion requests are separate, and the developer does not determine providers' retention periods.

Toss and advertising providers may process device and advertising identifiers, usage and ad interactions for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.   • Usage metrics: Apps in Toss Analytics measures app starts, game starts and completions, completion of three rounds during one app execution, actual interstitial impressions, and starting the next round after an ad. The SDK's automatically included anonymous identifier and fixed event names are sent to Toss. We do not attach player names, round identifiers, results, scores or saved game records to these events. The developer uses aggregated Toss console metrics to understand return visits and usage before and after ads. When someone opens the mini-app from a shared link, only the game type is read to suggest that game on the home screen; it is not stored. Please make sure the people named agree before you share.
  • Review requests: after two newly completed rounds, the app calls the Toss review request once per device from the home screen. Only the time of the request is stored in Toss Storage, and resetting game data does not remove it. Toss decides whether the review sheet appears, and the app does not receive ratings or whether a review was written.

Purchase delivery records are stored separately from game records; clearing the mini-app's storage removes the device copy. Payments, refunds and transaction retention follow Toss and app-marketplace policies. See the Toss Privacy Policy and relevant in-app notices for data categories, retention, international processing, rights and advertising privacy choices. Where Google ads are served, Google's Privacy Policy also applies. The developer does not determine providers' retention periods.
