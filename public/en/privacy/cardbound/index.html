Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: October 3, 2026
Android package: com.binarytechkr.cardgame

Binarytech provides Cardbound Expedition. Game progress and preferences stay on your device. There are no ads, and a one-time Google Play purchase unlocks the full game.

1. Operator and collection

Binarytech is the operator and privacy contact: binarytech804@gmail.com. The game has no account registration, separate login, advertising, analytics SDK or crash reporting to a developer server. We do not collect or share personal information from gameplay through a developer server.

2. Information stored on your device

The current expedition, growth and game records, preferences and full game ownership are stored in app-specific storage. We cannot remotely access information that exists only on your device. Clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the game to remove the local copy. Your device or operating system backup service may keep another copy; its settings govern deletion and restoration.

3. Full game purchase and Google Play

The full game is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Google processes payment methods, the Play account and transaction records; we do not receive card numbers. The game uses Unity IAP and Google Play Billing to retrieve prices and purchase status and acknowledge purchases to unlock the full game. Purchase verification and restoration require an internet connection. The game does not transmit purchase-related information to a developer server or analytics service.

Purchases can be restored with the same Google Play account. Deleting app data does not delete Google Play order records or refund a purchase. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's payment and refund guidance for Google's processing, retention, international processing and privacy rights.

4. Third parties and children

We do not share locally stored game records with third parties. Google Play payment processing described above follows Google's service policies. The game is intended for people aged 13 and older and does not request children's names, dates of birth or contact details.

5. Support and your rights

If you email support, we process your sender address and message to respond and resolve your request. We delete them without undue delay once that purpose is fulfilled, except where retention is required by law or necessary for a dispute, and then only for the necessary scope and period. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or sensitive information.

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other rights under applicable law regarding personal information we process, contact binarytech804@gmail.com. Game records stored only on your device must be deleted on that device. For information Google processes, use Google's privacy tools and contact procedures.

6. Changes

We will update this page and its revision date when processing changes. Significant changes will be announced through the app or the public policy page.
